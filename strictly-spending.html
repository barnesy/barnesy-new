AI-Native Personal Finance

Here is an open source project that's AI-Native and Local-First for personal finance. You can analyze, budget, forecast and dream a little dream about your finances and future plans. All with free local Gemma models from Google.

The AI can create artifacts for planning and eventually for completing personal taxes. This project was mostly created with Gemini models, built specifically to leverage free local Gemma models.

Strictly Spending dashboard in action

The Architecture

Strictly Spending is a local-first desktop application built with Tauri v2, Rust, and SQLite for a high-performance native backend, combined with Vite, React, and TypeScript for the frontend.

The local AI chat is powered by Ollama. No server. No cloud. No account credentials. All your financial data lives securely in a local SQLite database on your machine.
