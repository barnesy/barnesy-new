About Me

I design the platforms behind AI products. That means pattern libraries, agent UX, and the design infrastructure other designers and engineers build on.

Today I lead the design system and AI tooling at an enterprise SaaS platform. Before that, I did similar work at startups, agencies, and as a consultant. Past collaborators include Disney, Intel, Weather Channel, Southern Company Gas, Kimberly Clark, and IHG.

I'm a designer who ships code, so the systems I design are the same ones I prototype and hand off. What I'm best at: turning a fuzzy "we need an AI feature" into a real platform of tokens, components, agents, and workflows.

Based in Atlanta.
